## 4. SHORT-FORM VIDEO HOOKS (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Post 1-3/day. Each clip 7-15s. Vertical 9:16. Hook in the first 1.5 seconds or you're dead.

Use your existing `video_promo1_886x1920_30s.mp4` for the launch post, then use these hooks for ongoing content:

### 10 Hook Scripts

1. **"POV: you're addicted to Wordle but want more"** → cut to 5 game modes flashing → end on Plummet logo + "free, link in bio"

2. **"This word game has a mode nobody else has"** → 1s pause → cut to Word Runner gameplay (running + collecting letters) → "it's called Word Runner. it's free."

3. **"Rate my 7-day streak 🔥"** → show streak badge → quick cut of best play → "you can't beat me. try."

4. **"Indie devs check in"** → screen-record yourself building a feature in 5 sec timelapse → "shipping a word game on app store. wish me luck"

5. **"Word games tier list"** → quick rank: Wordle S, Scrabble A, Words With Friends B → drop Plummet at S+ → "biased but fair"

6. **Satisfying gameplay loop** — no voiceover, just clean gameplay + lo-fi music + onscreen text "no ads. no paywalls. just words." → 8 seconds

7. **"Things in this game that shouldn't be free"** → list: 5 modes, daily challenges, music player, leaderboards, no ads → "but it is. download Plummet."

8. **"You have 10 seconds. spell as many words as you can"** → film yourself playing Speed Round → end on score → "beat me. I dare you"

9. **"Got rejected from the App Store 3 times. here's the version that finally shipped"** → show gameplay → relatable indie founder content does numbers

10. **"Word game streak: day 1 vs day 30"** → split screen of score progression → motivational arc

### Posting cadence
- Week 1: 3 posts/day for 7 days = 21 swings at the algo
- Hashtags: `#wordgame #wordle #wordsearch #brainteaser #puzzlegame #indiegame #appstore #gamesoftiktok`
- Always pin a comment with your store link
- Reply to every single comment in the first 2 hours — drives reach

### Reddit posts (1-shot drops, don't spam)
- **r/WordGames** — "I built a word game with 5 modes including an endless runner. Free, no ads. Looking for feedback." Link to TestFlight or store.
- **r/iosgaming** + **r/AndroidGaming** — same energy, mention it's free
- **r/SideProject** — share the journey, not just the product
- **r/IndieDev** — same
