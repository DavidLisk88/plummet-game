Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Plummet ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to collecting only what we need to make the game work and to be transparent about every piece of data involved.

1. Information You Provide (Optional)

The App can be played without an account. If you choose to create one, we may collect:

  • Email address — used solely for account login, password reset, and (if enabled) account deletion verification.
  • Username / display name — shown on leaderboards and shared score cards. This is chosen by you and may be a pseudonym.
  • Password — stored as a one-way hash; we never see or store your raw password.

2. Information Generated by Gameplay

When you play, the App stores:

  • Game scores, XP, level, coins, achievements, streaks, and unlocked items
  • Words found and challenges completed
  • Player profile preferences

This data is stored locally on your device and, if you have an account, also synced to our backend (powered by Supabase) so it can appear on leaderboards and persist across devices.

3. Push Notifications (Optional)

If you grant notification permission, we store a device push token to send you reminders such as the daily Word of the Day or streak alerts. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings; revoking deletes the token from your device.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your real name, phone number, or address
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • We do not collect payment information (the App is free)
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell or rent any data to third parties — ever

5. Service Providers

We use the following processors to operate the App. Each is bound by their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, leaderboards (privacy policy)
  • Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications when you opt in
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — for app distribution (subject to their own policies)

6. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Play anonymously — no account required for single-player gameplay
  • Access your data — request a copy via email below
  • Delete your account — visit our account deletion page or email us; we will permanently remove your account and associated data within 30 days
  • Disable notifications — via your device settings
  • Opt out of leaderboards — by playing without an account

8. Data Retention

Account data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we erase your data from our active systems within 30 days. Backups are rotated and overwritten within 90 days.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all network communication, hashed passwords, and access controls on our backend. No system is perfectly secure; you should use a unique password and keep your device updated.

10. International Users

Our backend is hosted in the United States. By using the App you consent to your data being processed there. We comply with GDPR and CCPA regarding access, correction, and deletion rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
